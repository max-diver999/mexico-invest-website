Quintana Roo municipalities tightened STR registration in June 2026. July brought a parallel SAT focus in Playa del Carmen: matching platform-reported rental inflows to taxpayer IDs for non-resident property owners.

This is not a ban on foreign STR, it is an enforcement wave on owners who treated Airbnb deposits as tax-free because withholding appeared on platform statements.

## What enforcement looks like in July 2026

The mechanism is data matching rather than inspection: platform-reported host income is cross-checked against RFC filings and the municipal lodging register, which is why an unregistered listing is now findable from a desk rather than from the street.

| Signal | Indicative impact | Owner action |
|---|---|---|
| Platform vs RFC mismatch | Audit letter | Reconcile with accountant |
| Missing municipal lodging ID | Municipal fine stack | Register before scaling nights |
| No trailing CFDI | ISR basis gap on sale | Rebuild document vault |
| US Schedule E not filed | IRS exposure | Cross-border CPA review |
| Manager without tax clause | Owner liability | Rewrite contract |

## Compliance stack (four layers)

Short-term-rental compliance in Solidaridad is not one requirement but four independent ones, administered by four different bodies that do not check each other's work. A unit can satisfy three and be shut down by the fourth. The layers run from the property outward: the condominium regime's own rules on nightly letting, the HOA's régimen de condominio clause, municipal lodging registration under Quintana Roo rules, and the federal stack of RFC registration, monthly and annual SAT filings, and CFDI discipline. Break any one of them and the gross yield in the marketing collapses under fines and platform takedowns.

1. **Ownership**: fideicomiso beneficiary rights ([Can Foreigners Buy](/guides/can-foreigners-buy-property-mexico/)) Each layer has a different owner, the notario, the HOA, the municipality and SAT, and none of them tells the others when you are missing one.
2. **HOA**: regime de condominio STR clause
3. **Municipal**: Quintana Roo lodging registration
4. **Federal**: RFC, SAT monthly/annual filings, CFDI discipline

Break any layer and gross yield marketing collapses under fines.

US side: [Form 1116 Foreign Tax Credit Guide](/guides/form-1116-foreign-tax-credit-mexico-rental/) · [US Taxes on Mexico Rental Property](/guides/us-taxes-mexico-rental-property/).

## How does this comparison stack up for Mexico investors?

A compliant Playa 1BR still nets 4.3% to 4.5% after management, HOA and the trust fee. An interrupted one loses a season rather than paying a fine, and the season it loses is the November-to-April window that carries the year.

| Item | Indicative annual USD | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mexican accountant retainer | $800–1,500 | Non-resident STR common |
| US cross-border CPA | $500–1,200 | Schedule E + Form 1116 |
| Municipal lodging fees | Varies by municipality | Building-specific |
| Platform withholding | 10–20% of gross | Not final tax |
| Audit penalty risk | Often 5-figure MXN plus back taxes | Avoidable with RFC |

## What checklist should run before you sign?

For new inventory the addition to the standard list is the developer's written commitment on the régimen de condominio's rental clause, because that clause is set at registration before there is an HOA to lobby and it decides whether a nightly rental business exists at all.

- [ ] Confirm RFC status with manager in writing
- [ ] Pull trailing-12 platform statements vs SAT filings
- [ ] Verify municipal lodging registration number
- [ ] Update US Schedule E and Form 1116 with CPA
- [ ] Review HOA minutes for STR votes in last 24 months
