## Compliance stack unchanged but enforced

The Quintana Roo framework has not changed; what changed is that each layer is now being checked. All four steps below must hold together, and an owner with private title and a bank trust but no building permission or ISH registration is still operating outside the rules.

Quintana Roo framework still applies:

1. Private title + fideicomiso
2. Building STR permission
3. [Quintana Roo Lodging Tax Registration](/guides/quintana-roo-lodging-tax-registration-guide/)
4. SAT RFC if renting: [Non-Resident RFC Guide](/guides/non-resident-tax-id-rfc-guide/)

## Broker takeaway

Island STR investors treating Holbox like Playa-without-cars fail DD, ferry cutoffs and water caps are operational constraints, not footnotes. Budget legal and manager fees before comparing sticker to mainland. Two island-specific costs make the point: water trucks on fringe north-road builds ran $800 to $1,400 a month in the dry April 2026 samples, and whale-shark season lifts ADR 15% to 22% from May through September, so a buyer underwriting only the winter high season misses both the mid-year revenue and the mid-year cost.
