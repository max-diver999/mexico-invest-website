## Ferry logistics and guest experience

The ferry is the operational risk that defines this market. Door-to-door from CUN is closer to two hours than the "25 minutes" in listing copy, the last crossing strands guests who miss it, and hurricane winds suspend service entirely. Underwrite the friction honestly: it costs you bookings from families and buys you guests who wanted an island.

Ferry dependency shapes both the guest experience and the operating cost in ways a mainland model does not capture. Every guest, contractor, appliance and delivery crosses from Puerto Juárez or Gran Puerto, which adds time and cost to every maintenance call and makes same-day emergency response harder to guarantee. A ferry crossing runs roughly 20 to 30 minutes at $10 to $20 return per person, and it filters the guest: visitors who accept it tend to stay longer and rate the island experience highly, so average length of stay runs above Playa's 3 to 4 nights while the addressable market is correspondingly narrower.

Operators coordinate:

- CUN arrival → Puerto Juárez taxi → ferry → golf cart check-in
- Last ferry misses, backup hotel night in Cancún eats margin
- Hurricane wind, ferry suspension days

Properties advertising "Cancún airport 25 minutes" usually mean to ferry dock, not door-to-door island check-in, underwrite guest friction honestly.

Four approvals have to line up before the first guest, and they are issued by four different bodies: the building's reglamento, Quintana Roo lodging tax registration, SAT income reporting, and, for US owners, Schedule E at home. Missing any one of them is a fine or a delisting, not a warning.

## Residential zoning vs STR-permitted buildings

Rental permission on Isla Mujeres is not binary. A hotel-zone registered building is fully permitted and prices at a premium; a residential building with explicit HOA authorisation is compliant but discounted 3–5% when the minutes are ambiguous; a residential building with silence in the minutes is a risk priced as though it were permission. Get the land-use certificate and a dated HOA letter, not an assurance.

Isla Mujeres has three STR permission tiers within residential zones, not binary legal/illegal. Buyers must distinguish **hotel-zone permitted**, **residential with HOA approval**, and **residential prohibited** before purchase.

**Zoning clarity matrix:**

| Building type | STR permission level | Indicative purchase discount if unclear | Verification document |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hotel-zone registered | Fully permitted, municipal lodging ID routine | No discount, premium even | Certificate of land use |
| Residential with HOA explicit approval | Compliant if assembly minutes confirm | 3-5% discount if minutes ambiguous | HOA STR authorization letter dated within 6 months |
| Residential with silent HOA | Gray zone, vulnerable to future ban | 8-12% discount appropriate | Request 3 years assembly minutes |
| Residential with explicit ban | Not investable for STR | Often 15-25% below comparable STR-legal units | HOA reglamento STR prohibition clause |

**Why silent HOA is investment trap:** Buildings without clear STR stance in reglamento or recent assembly minutes can flip to prohibited after one neighbor complaint. Buyers assuming "no rule means allowed" discover post-closing that HOA votes STR ban with 6-month grandfather window insufficient to recoup closing costs.

**Pre-offer verification checklist:**
1. Request HOA reglamento (bylaws) in Spanish or English
2. Ask for last 3 assembly minutes, search for "Airbnb," "renta corta," "hospedaje"
3. Obtain written letter from HOA administrator confirming STR permission for that unit class
4. Cross-check with municipal lodging registry if publicly searchable
5. Budget legal review $300-600 for Spanish reglamento translation and interpretation

Foreign buyers who skip this step and rely on seller verbal "yes it's fine" account for 30-40% of post-purchase STR prohibition surprises in Solidaridad and island markets per attorney estimates.

Cross-reference HOA dynamics: [Short-Term Rental Rules Riviera Maya](/guides/short-term-rental-rules-riviera-maya/), mainland Playa assembly politics mirror island patterns, but Isla Mujeres residential density lower means fewer votes needed to flip HOA stance.
