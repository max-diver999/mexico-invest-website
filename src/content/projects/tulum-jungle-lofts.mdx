## Ownership and closing for foreigners

Foreign buyers use fideicomiso, mandatory in Tulum's coastal zone. At $165K, trust setup represents approximately 1.5–2.5% of purchase price, more manageable than at $98K entry points. Ensure the contract specifies trust formation timeline and that the fideicomiso covers the specific parcel number as surveyed.

| Getting to signature on $165K | |
|---|---|
| ISAI, 2–3% of assessed value | $3,300–$4,950 |
| Notarial and registry charges | $2,475–$4,125 |
| Trust formation | $2,500–$4,000 |

Foreign buyers close remotely on a power of attorney as a matter of routine here, with the trust registering 30 to 90 days after the unit is handed over.

## What checklist should run before you sign?

Jungle-parcel Tulum at $165K puts the agrarian question first. Require an ejido check running 500 metres beyond the parcel boundary rather than to the line itself, because communal claims in this zone frequently overlap adjoining land and a clean parcel next to a contested one is still a problem at resale. SEMARNAT clearance must be verified for the specific parcel rather than the development as a whole, the Tulum municipal licence confirmed as current, and pre-structural escrow exposure capped at 10-15% before the concrete is complete.

Nothing below is optional, and the order is deliberate, items one to three can end the purchase before the rest is worth paying for:

1. Title search: clear escritura, no ejido within 500m, no agrarian claim.
2. SEMARNAT: environmental clearance verified for specific parcel.
3. Construction permit: Tulum municipio licencia confirmed current.
4. Escrow: milestone structure, maximum 10–15% before concrete complete.
5. HOA pro forma: 5-year projection including natural-material maintenance reserves.
6. STR authorization: written HOA permission for short-term rentals.
7. ADR comps: jungle-loft Tulum Airbnb data, not Hotel Zone villa rates.
8. Attorney review: default terms, delay penalties, refund conditions.

The complete legal sequence is in [Due Diligence Mexico Real Estate](/guides/due-diligence-mexico-real-estate/).
