Quivira is a 1,850-acre master plan on the Pacific side of Los Cabos with a working hotel, a championship golf course and a marina already in the ground — which is a materially different proposition from a luxury project that promises those things in a brochure. ## Resale market and exit strategy

Expect a 12–36 month marketing period and a specialist broker. ## What red flags should pause this Mexico purchase?

The warning signs here are about the master plan rather than the unit: deferred maintenance on the golf course or marina, an MCA reserve that will not cover the amenity base, sales velocity that has stalled in the phase you are buying into, or discounting across standing inventory. Any of those means the infrastructure premium you are paying for is being funded out of new sales rather than out of operations.
