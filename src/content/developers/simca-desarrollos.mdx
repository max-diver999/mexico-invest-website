## Corporate structure and financial analysis

A boutique developer carries less balance sheet than a volume operator, and on a $300,000–850,000 purchase that is the number that matters when a build runs late. ## Escrow and payment protection

The better a developer's reputation, the more likely they are to argue that escrow is unnecessary — and the argument is worth nothing, because escrow protects against outcomes reputation cannot prevent. Keep the reservation small and refundable, put the 10–15% contract deposit with an independent agent, and release construction milestones only on an engineer's sign-off.

Premium developers often resist escrow citing track record, maintain escrow discipline regardless of SIMCA reputation or 101 Park delivery evidence.

| Payment milestone | Protection requirement |
|---|---|
| Reservation deposit | Refundable structure |
| Contract deposit (10–15%) | Independent escrow agent |
| Construction milestones | Engineer sign-off required |
| Pre-delivery (final 25%) | Certificate of occupancy |

Premium trap: "We don't need escrow, we're established", insist on independent escrow regardless of developer tier or track record claims.

## Financing and premium purchase considerations

Above roughly $500,000 the Mexican financing market effectively closes to foreign buyers, and premium SIMCA projects are priced squarely in that zone. Cross-border lenders exist but concentrate below this level, and Mexican banks lend to residents on documented Mexican income rather than to non-resident investors. That leaves three practical routes: cash, a US mortgage cash-out against existing real estate at home, or developer financing where SIMCA offers it — which at premium price points some developers do at genuinely competitive rates. Model the all-in borrowing cost of each against the property's net yield before assuming leverage improves the return.

| Financing option | Premium project application |
|---|---|
| Cash purchase | Standard for premium market |
| US mortgage cash-out | Leverage existing real estate |
| Developer financing | Rare but potential at premium tier |
| Mexican private banking | Ultra-high-net-worth only |

Premium strategy: Cash deployment enables faster closing and negotiation leverage, finance in US market typically cheaper than Mexico premium developer rates.
