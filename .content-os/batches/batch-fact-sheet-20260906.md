## Indicative price bands, source and method

All price bands are **asking-price observations**, not valuations and not
closed-sale data. Collected from public listing portals (realtor.com
international, Properstar, Century 21 Mexico) in September 2026 for two and
three bedroom homes in ordinary condition. Every table carrying them is
labelled indicative in the body and repeated in the page footer disclaimer.

They are Tier C by the content-os hierarchy, which is why they are presented as
bands rather than points, and why no yield or return is derived from them
anywhere in this batch.

## What this batch deliberately does not claim

- No MORE Group performance figures, deal counts, client shares or growth
  forecasts. None appear in any of the six pages.
- No liquidity verdicts on any market.
- No yield projections built on the indicative bands above.
- No claim that any specific bank, notario or agency is cheapest or best.
